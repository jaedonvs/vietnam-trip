# 🇻🇳 Vietnam Trip Plan — Don & Katelyn

**Last updated:** May 2026  
**Target dates:** Last week of June → First week of July 2026 (10-15 days)  
**Travellers:** 2 (couple, both 24)  
**From:** South Africa (Johannesburg)  
**Budget style:** Budget-friendly, good value  
**Route:** North to South — Hanoi → Ninh Binh → Hue → Hoi An → Da Nang → HCMC (B2) or → Nha Trang → HCMC (B1)

## 🍜 Option B1: The Deep Dive (15 Days)

> 💡 **Why skip Halong?** July has storm risk — cruises get cancelled, views are misty. Instead, sink into Ninh Binh (Halong Bay on land) and Hoi An. Added HCMC back in — too good to miss!

### Day 1 — ✈️ Fly JHB → Hanoi
- Qatar Airways via Doha: ~20h total, ~R12,300 return pp
- Arrive Hanoi, get Viettel SIM at airport (~200K VND ≈ R150 for 30 days data)
- Grab taxi to Old Quarter (~R80)
- Evening: Bun Cha Huong Lien (Obama's spot, ~R60 for 2) + Bia Hoi corner (draft beer ~R8/glass!)
- **Stay:** Old Quarter guesthouse ~R300/night

### Day 2 — 🏙️ Hanoi Deep Dive
- Morning: guided street food tour by scooter (~3h, covers 6-8 dishes, ~R250pp)
- Midday: egg coffee at Café Giang (where it was invented), Train Street coffee watching trains pass
- Afternoon: Hoa Lo Prison (Hanoi Hilton) — sobering but essential (~R50pp)
- Evening: water puppet show (~R70pp) + Old Quarter bar crawl
- **Stay:** Same guesthouse ~R300/night

### Day 3 — 🛖 Hanoi → Ninh Binh
- Morning bus from Hanoi (~2h, ~R80pp)
- Rent scooter in Ninh Binh (~R100/day)
- **Trang An Grottoes** ⭐ (boat tour 2-3h, ~R150pp) — UNESCO site, stunning limestone caves
- Evening: **Mua Caves viewpoint** (500 steps, go for sunset! Entry ~R75pp)
- **Stay:** Homestay in Tam Coc area ~R300/night

### Day 4 — 🛖 Ninh Binh (full day)
- Morning: **Bai Dinh Pagoda** (largest religious complex in SE Asia, free entry, stunning)
- Afternoon: **Bich Dong Pagoda** (cave temple in the hillside, free), scooter through Tam Coc rice paddies
- Lazy river afternoon. Pack bags for evening train.
- **Stay:** Same homestay ~R300/night

### Day 5 — 🚂 Ninh Binh → Hue (Sleeper Train)
- Morning: last Ninh Binh wander, coffee by the river
- Afternoon/evening: overnight sleeper train (~11h)
- Book soft-sleeper 4-berth on Laman Express (~R1,250pp) — nicer than basic
- Pack snacks! Saves a hotel night.
- Book on **12go.asia** or **baolau.com**

### Day 6 — 🏯 Hue (Arrive ~08:00)
- **Imperial City** (entry ~R100pp) — 2-3h exploring the citadel, palace ruins
- **Royal Tombs** by scooter — Tu Duc (peaceful, lakeside) + Khai Dinh (ornate, hilltop). Tour ~R350
- Dinner: **Bun Bo Hue** (Hue's legendary spicy beef noodle soup, ~R30/bowl)
- Perfume River sunset walk
- **Stay:** Hostel ~R150pp, private ~R350/night

### Day 7 — 🏍️ Hue → Hoi An (Hai Van Pass)
- **Easy-rider tour** (~R700pp) — you ride pillion with a local driver (safest way!)
- Stop at **Elephant Springs** for a swim in natural pools
- Photo stops at the summit overlooking the coast — one of the world's best coastal roads
- Arrive Hoi An late afternoon
- Evening: wander lantern-lit Old Town at night ✨
- Old Town ticket: ~R75pp (valid 5 days, covers 5 attractions)
- **Stay:** Homestay/guesthouse ~R350/night

### Day 8 — 🏮 Hoi An — Old Town & Tailoring
- Morning: Old Town walking tour — Japanese Covered Bridge, Assembly Halls, old merchant houses
- **Get measured for tailored clothes first thing!** (suit ~R1,500-3,000, dress ~R800-2,000, ready in 24-48h)
- Afternoon: **Cooking class** (~R400pp) — market tour + cook 4 dishes
- Evening: lantern boat ride on the river (~R30)
- **Stay:** Same ~R350/night

### Day 9 — 🏮 Hoi An — Beach Day
- **An Bang Beach** — 4km from town, free, rent lounger ~R30
- Swim, read, chill all morning
- Afternoon: fitting for tailored clothes, final Hoi An wandering
- Don't leave without trying **banh mi** (best in Vietnam!) and **cao lau** noodles — only made in Hoi An
- **Stay:** Same ~R350/night

### Day 10 — 🏮 Hoi An → Nha Trang
- Early morning: bike ride through rice paddies at sunrise (bike rental ~R30/d, magical light)
- Pick up tailored clothes — final fitting
- Late morning: Grab to Danang airport
- Fly Danang → Nha Trang/Cam Ranh (~1h, ~R900pp)
- **Stay:** Beachside guesthouse ~R400/night

### Day 11 — 🏖️ Nha Trang — Beach & Mud Baths
- Best July weather in Vietnam! ⭐
- Morning: beach chill, swim, soak up the sun
- Afternoon: **Thap Ba mud baths** (~R150pp) — uniquely Vietnamese, weird and wonderful
- Evening: fresh seafood dinner on the beach (~R150-250pp)
- **Stay:** Same ~R400/night

### Day 12 — 🏖️ Nha Trang — Vinpearl or Chill Day
- **Option A:** Vinpearl Land — amusement park on an island, cable car over the ocean (~R600pp)
- **Option B:** Doc Let Beach day trip (40min north, pristine, quiet)
- Or just another beach day — no judgement!
- **Stay:** Same ~R400/night

### Day 13 — ✈️ Nha Trang → Ho Chi Minh City
- Fly Cam Ranh → HCMC (~1.5h, ~R900pp)
- Check into District 1 (backpacker area)
- Afternoon: **War Remnants Museum** (~R45pp) — powerful, essential visit
- Evening: **Bui Vien Street** — backpacker bars, live music, cheap Bia Saigon. The energy is unreal!
- **Stay:** District 1 ~R400/night

### Day 14 — 🌆 HCMC — Cu Chi Tunnels + City
- Morning: **Cu Chi Tunnels** half-day tour (~R300pp incl. transport) — crawl through Viet Cong tunnels
- Afternoon: **Ben Thanh Market** (bargain hard — start at 50%), Independence Palace
- Coffee culture crawl — ca phe sua da (Vietnamese iced coffee is life)
- Street food marathon for dinner
- **Stay:** Same ~R400/night

### Day 15 — 🛶 Mekong Delta → ✈️ Fly Home
- Early morning: **Mekong Delta day tour** (~R350-500pp) — floating markets at dawn, boat through coconut groves, fruit orchards, local lunch
- Back to HCMC by ~15:00
- Evening flight HCMC → Doha → JHB
- Last bowl of pho at the airport — see you next time Vietnam! 🇻🇳

## ⚡ Option B2: The Express Deep Dive (10 Days)

> ⚡ **10 days, zero fluff — now with a beach day!** This is B1 on fast-forward — every highlight, no lazy days. Skip Nha Trang but get a proper beach afternoon at My Khe (Da Nang) — and you're 10min from the airport the next morning for your HCMC flight. Best for annual leave crushers.

### Day 1 — ✈️ Fly JHB → Hanoi
- Qatar Airways via Doha: ~20h total, ~R12,300 return pp
- Arrive Hanoi, get Viettel SIM at airport (~R150)
- Grab taxi to Old Quarter (~R80)
- Evening: Bun Cha Huong Lien (Obama's spot, ~R60 for 2) + Bia Hoi corner (draft beer ~R8/glass!)
- Get an early night — tomorrow's big
- **Stay:** Old Quarter guesthouse ~R300/night

### Day 2 — 🏙️ Hanoi → Ninh Binh
- Morning: quick Hanoi hits — egg coffee at Café Giang, Hoa Lo Prison (~1.5h, ~R50pp)
- Midday: bus to Ninh Binh (~2h, ~R80pp)
- Afternoon: **Trang An boat caves** ⭐ (2-3h rowboat, UNESCO!, ~R150pp)
- Rent scooter (~R100/d). Stay in Tam Coc.
- **Stay:** Tam Coc homestay ~R300/night

### Day 3 — 🛖 Ninh Binh → 🚂 Sleeper Train to Hue

- Mid-morning: **Bai Dinh Pagoda** (largest in SE Asia, free)
- Afternoon: relax by the river, pack
- Evening: sleeper train to Hue on Laman Express (~11h, ~R1,250pp). Pack snacks!
- **Saves a hotel night!**

### Day 4 — 🏯 Hue → 🏍️ Hoi An (via Hai Van Pass)
- Arrive Hue ~08:00. Quick **Imperial City** visit (2h, ~R100pp)
- Then: **easy-rider to Hoi An** (~R700pp) via the **Hai Van Pass** ⭐ — one of the world's best coastal roads
- Swim at Elephant Springs
- Arrive Hoi An late afternoon. Evening: lantern-lit Old Town ✨

### Day 5 — 🏮 Hoi An — Old Town & Tailoring
- Morning: Old Town walking tour — Japanese Covered Bridge, Assembly Halls (Old Town ticket ~R75pp)

- Afternoon: **cooking class** (~R400pp) — market tour + cook 4 dishes
- Evening: lantern boat on the river (~R30), banh mi & cao lau feast
- **Stay:** Same ~R350/night

### Day 6 — 🏖️ Hoi An → Da Nang (My Khe Beach)
- Morning: pick up tailored clothes — final fitting
- Midday: Grab to Da Nang (~45min, ~R200)
- Check into a **beachfront hotel on My Khe Beach** — one of Vietnam's best city beaches, white sand, calm water ⭐
- Afternoon: full beach chill, swim, seafood dinner on the sand
- You're now **10min from Da Nang airport** — no rush tomorrow!
- **Stay:** Da Nang beachfront ~R400/night

### Day 7 — ✈️ Da Nang → HCMC
- Morning: last swim at My Khe if you're keen
- Quick 10min Grab to Da Nang airport
- Fly Da Nang → HCMC (~1.5h, ~R900pp)
- Afternoon: **War Remnants Museum** (~R45pp) — powerful, essential
- Evening: **Bui Vien Street** — bars, live music, Bia Saigon 🔥
- **Stay:** District 1 ~R400/night

### Day 8 — 🌆 HCMC — Cu Chi + City
- Morning: **Cu Chi Tunnels** half-day tour (~R300pp) — crawl through Viet Cong tunnels
- Afternoon: **Ben Thanh Market** (bargain hard — start at 50%), Independence Palace
- Coffee culture crawl — ca phe sua da is life
- Street food marathon for dinner
- **Stay:** Same ~R400/night

### Day 9 — 🛶 Mekong Delta Day Trip
- Early morning: **Mekong Delta day tour** (~R350-500pp) — floating markets at dawn, coconut groves, fruit orchards, local lunch
- Back to HCMC by ~15:00
- Evening: last Vietnamese dinner, pack, one more ca phe sua da
- **Stay:** Same ~R400/night

### Day 10 — ✈️ Fly Home

- 10 days, 5 cities, zero regrets 🇻🇳

### 💡 Money-Saving Tips
- **Street food is the best food** — pho ~R15-20, banh mi ~R15, beer ~R10
- **Book hostels/guesthouses in person** — often cheaper than online
- **Use Grab** (Vietnam's Uber) — always cheaper than flagged taxis
- **ATMs give best rates** — withdraw VND, avoid currency exchange at airports
- **Sleeper trains = free accommodation** — overnight routes save a night's stay
- **July = low season in north/south** — cheaper accommodation & fewer crowds

## 📋 Visa Info (South African Citizens)

- **E-visa required** for SA passport holders
- Apply online: https://evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn/
- Cost: ~$25 USD (~R470) for 90 days, single entry
- Processing: ~3 working days
- **New e-visa option:** 90 days multiple entry also available (~$50 USD / ~R940)

## 📱 Practical Tips

### Getting Around
- **Grab app** — Vietnam's Uber (download before you go)
- **12go.asia** & **baolau.com** — book trains/buses in advance
- **Scooter rental** ~R80-100/day — but only if comfortable on roads!
- **Overnight sleeper buses** — budget option for long routes, ~R200-350

### Money
- **Currency:** Vietnamese Dong (VND)
- **1 ZAR ≈ 1,400 VND** (check current rate)
- Cash is king outside HCMC/Hanoi — carry VND
- ATMs widely available, max withdrawal ~2M VND (~R1,400) per transaction
- Notify your bank you're travelling to Vietnam!

### SIM Card
- **Viettel** is best for coverage nationwide (including rural areas)
- ~200,000 VND (~R150) for 30-day tourist SIM with data
- Buy at airport on arrival or any Viettel shop

### Food Budget (per meal)
- Street food: R15-30 per dish
- Local restaurant: R50-100 per meal
- Mid-range restaurant: R100-200 per meal
- Beer (Bia Hoi): R8-15 per glass (cheapest beer in the world!)
