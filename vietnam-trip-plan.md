# Vietnam Itinerary — 21 June to 2 July

## Trip Overview

- **Travellers:** Couple, mid-twenties, first time in Southeast Asia
- **Dates:** Arrive Hanoi 21 June (midday) · Depart Ho Chi Minh City 2 July (20:00)
- **Duration:** 12 days in-country
- **Routing:** Open-jaw — fly into Hanoi (HAN), fly out of Ho Chi Minh City (SGN)
- **Budget:** Comfortable (boutique hotels, mix of dining)
- **Vibe:** Big shoppers & foodies · Imperial & temple history · Beach · Adventure · Nightlife
- **History focus:** Imperial/temple primary · Key Vietnam War sites only
- **Key alert:** Dragon Bridge fire & water show — Friday, Saturday & Sunday nights at 21:00. You are in Da Nang on **Saturday 27 AND Sunday 28 June** (both nights!). Do not miss this.

---

## Weather by Region

| Region | Dates | Conditions | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanoi | 21–22 Jun | 34–36°C, afternoon thunderstorms | Medium — outdoor mornings only |
| Ha Long Bay | 23–24 Jun | 30–32°C, misty | Low — mist looks magical, cruises run |
| Hoi An & Da Nang | 25–28 Jun | 32–34°C, dry season | Low — best weather of the trip |
| Ho Chi Minh City | 29 Jun–2 Jul | 30–33°C, heavy afternoon rain | High — outdoor activities before noon only |

**HCMC rain pattern:** Clear mornings → heavy rain 14:00–17:00 → clears again by 19:00. Plan all outdoor activities before noon.

*Tags: history · shopping · food*

#### ⚠️ Ha Long Bay Backup Plan: Ninh Binh (Trang An)

If the Ha Long Bay cruise is cancelled due to severe weather, Ninh Binh is the ideal substitute — often called "Ha Long Bay on land." Limestone karsts rising from rice paddies instead of water. UNESCO World Heritage Site. About 2 hours from Hanoi by car.

**Why it works perfectly:** It has karst scenery AND imperial history (Hoa Lu was Vietnam's first capital, 968 AD) — fits the trip theme beautifully.

**What to do:**
- ✅ **Trang An Boat Tour (3–4 hrs)** — rowing through caves, karst gorges and paddy landscapes, serene and spectacular
- ✅ **Hoa Lu Ancient Capital — temples** — Vietnam's first capital city, two 10th-century temple complexes (Dinh Tien Hoang & Le Hoan) built into the karst mountains
- **Mua Cave Viewpoint** — climb 500 steps for panoramic views over the entire Trang An valley
- **Bich Dong Pagoda** — ancient pagoda built inside a cave, three chamber levels

**How to restructure Days 3–4 if this happens:**
- Day 3: Ninh Binh day trip from Hanoi (hire a private car, ~$80 all day)
- Day 4: Relaxed Hanoi morning + extra shopping + evening flight to Da Nang, arriving in Hoi An with a bonus day

### Day 5 — Thursday 25 June
**Hoi An — Temples, Tailoring, Leather & Food**
*Tags: history · shopping · food*

#### Morning — Temple + Ancient Town + Order Tailoring
- ✅ **Japanese Covered Bridge + Ancient Town stroll** — before 09:00, cooler and far less crowded, Tan Ky Old House worth the entry
- 🌟 **★ Phuc Kien Assembly Hall (Fujian Assembly Hall)** — the most beautiful temple in Hoi An. Chinese-Vietnamese hall dedicated to the Goddess of the Sea. Enormous incense spirals hanging from the ceiling, ornate red altars, thick incense smoke. Go early when light comes through the entrance gate. Unmissable.
- ✅ **Izi Tailor — order custom pyjamas** — your bold pick, 24–48 hr turnaround
- ✅ **I Love Fashion — order clothes** — second tailor stop, order today, collect Day 7

- ✅ **Quan Cao Iau Ba Le — white rose dumplings** — can only be made authentically in Hoi An, non-negotiable
- **Com Linh — duck specialist** — your note, specialises in duck dishes, good local lunch spot

#### Shopping — Leather & Markets
- ✅ **Hoan Leather Store — tabi shoes & bag making** — your bold pick, watch the process, buy tabi, one of the most unique shopping experiences in Vietnam
- **Da Bao Real Leather, Leather Store 19, Song Leather** — your leather notes, browse after Hoan
- **Cocobox — café, market, fashion** — good for afternoon browse and coffee

#### Afternoon — Activity

- **An Bang Beach — sundowners at Soul Kitchen** — cycle from town (50,000 VND), cold beers on the sand

#### Evening

### Day 6 — Friday 26 June
**Hue — Imperial Day Trip**
*Tags: history*

**Travel:** Private driver over Hai Van Pass · Arrange through hotel tonight · ~$70–90 for the full day

#### Morning — The Drive + Hue
- ✅ **Depart Hoi An 07:30 over Hai Van Pass** — stop at the summit for panoramic views of Da Nang Bay and the South China Sea, one of Vietnam's great viewpoints
- ✅ **Hue Imperial Citadel + Forbidden Purple City** — 2+ hours, former seat of the Nguyen emperors, go in the morning before peak heat
- ✅ **Thien Mu Pagoda** — 7-storey pagoda on the Perfume River, 20 mins from the Citadel, iconic

#### Food
- ✅ **Bun Bo Hue — lunch at a local spot** — spicy lemongrass beef noodles, only properly made here, arguably better than pho, find a plastic-stool local place not a tourist restaurant

#### Afternoon
- ✅ **Royal Tomb of Tu Duc** — most romantic of the royal tombs, forested grounds, atmospheric, feels lost in time
- **Drive back via Lang Co beach lagoon** — beautiful coastal stop, ask driver to stop for 20 minutes

**If it rains:** Citadel has covered corridors — still doable in light rain. Skip Royal Tombs if heavy rain, replace with Hue Royal Antiquities Museum (indoor). Worst case: cancel Hue entirely and have a second beach day in Hoi An.

> **Tip:** Use a private driver, not a tour bus. You control the pace entirely. Your hotel can arrange for ~$70–90 all day.

## Practical Information

### Before You Go
- **Vietnam e-visa** — apply at evisa.gov.vn, $25, takes ~3 working days. Do this immediately.
- **Flights** — book open-jaw: fly into Hanoi (HAN), fly out of Ho Chi Minh City (SGN). Emirates via Dubai or Qatar Airways via Doha from Johannesburg, ~16–18 hrs total.
- **Domestic flights** — Hanoi → Da Nang and Da Nang → Ho Chi Minh City. Book on VietJet or Bamboo Airways in advance, ~R600–900 per sector.
- **Ha Long Bay cruise** — book immediately, good boats sell out weeks ahead.
- **Travel insurance** — ensure it covers adventure activities (kayaking, tunnels).
- **Vaccinations** — Hepatitis A/B and Typhoid recommended.

### Apps to Download
- **Grab** — Southeast Asia's Uber, use for all transport in cities
- **Google Translate** — offline Vietnamese language pack
- **Maps.me** — offline maps
- **VietJet / Bamboo Airways** — domestic flight apps

### Money
- Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). 1 ZAR ≈ ~1,200 VND
- ATMs widely available in all cities
- Cash preferred for street food, markets, tuk-tuks
- Cards accepted at boutique hotels and restaurants

### Getting Around
- **Cities:** Grab app for everything. Never hail a motorbike taxi off the street.
- **Hoi An:** Rent bicycles (~50,000 VND/day) for An Bang Beach
- **Ha Long Bay:** transfers included with cruise booking
- **Hue day trip:** hire a private car through your hotel (~$70–90)

### Budget Guide (per person, comfortable level)
| Category | Daily Estimate |
|---|---|
| Boutique accommodation | $50–80/night |
| Food (mix of local + restaurants) | $20–35/day |
| Activities & transport | $20–30/day |
| **Total per person** | **~$90–145/day** |

12-day trip ≈ **$1,100–1,700 per person** excluding international flights.
