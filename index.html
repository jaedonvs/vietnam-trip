🍜 Option B1: The Deep Dive

Fewer stops, more depth + HCMC — the best of Vietnam without rushing, 15 days
2 nights Ninh Binh 3 nights Hoi An 2 nights HCMC Mekong Delta No Halong Bay
R59,000budget for 2
▼
💡 Why skip Halong? July has storm risk — cruises get cancelled, views are misty. Instead, spend that time really sinking into Ninh Binh (Halong Bay on land) and Hoi An. If you want a boat day, do a Lan Ha Bay day-cruise from Cat Ba — cheaper, fewer tourists, similar scenery.
Day 1
✈️ Fly JHB → Hanoi
Arrive Hanoi, get Viettel SIM (~R150) at airport, Grab taxi to Old Quarter (~R80). Evening wander — Bun Cha Huong Lien (Obama's spot, ~R60 for 2) + Bia Hoi corner (draft beer ~R8/glass!)
SIM: R150 · Grab: R80 · Accom: R300 · Food: R200
Day 2
🏙️ Hanoi Deep Dive
Morning: guided street food tour by scooter (~3h, covers 6-8 dishes). Midday: egg coffee at Café Giang, Train Street coffee watching trains pass. Afternoon: Hoa Lo Prison (Hanoi Hilton) — sobering but essential. Evening: water puppet show + Old Quarter bar crawl
Accom: R300 · Food tour: R250pp · Hoa Lo: R50pp · Water puppets: R70pp · Food: R200
Day 3
🛖 Hanoi → Ninh Binh
Morning bus from Hanoi (~2h, ~R80pp). Rent scooter (~R100/d). Trang An boat caves ⭐ — 2-3h through limestone caves by rowboat (UNESCO!). Evening: sunset at Mua Caves viewpoint (500 steps, totally worth it). Stay in Tam Coc homestay
Bus: R80pp · Accom: R300 · Scooter: R100 · Trang An: R150pp · Mua Caves: R75pp · Food: R200
Day 4
🛖 Ninh Binh (full day)
Morning: Bai Dinh Pagoda (largest religious complex in SE Asia, free entry, stunning). Afternoon: Bich Dong Pagoda (cave temple in the hillside), scooter through Tam Coc rice paddies. Lazy river afternoon. Pack bags for evening train.
Accom: R300 · Scooter: R100 · Food: R200
Day 5
🚂 Ninh Binh → Hue (Sleeper Train)
Morning: last Ninh Binh wander, coffee by the river. Afternoon/evening: overnight sleeper train (~11h). Book Laman Express soft-sleeper 4-berth for comfort. Pack snacks! Saves a hotel night.
Train: R1,250pp · Food: R150
Day 6
🏯 Hue
Arrive ~08:00. Morning: Imperial City (2-3h exploring the citadel). Afternoon: scooter to Royal Tombs — Tu Duc (peaceful, lakeside) + Khai Dinh (ornate, hilltop). Dinner: Bun Bo Hue (Hue's legendary spicy beef noodle soup, ~R30/bowl). Perfume River sunset walk.
Accom: R350 · Imperial City: R100pp · Tombs tour: R350 · Food: R200
Day 7
🏍️ Hue → Hoi An (Hai Van Pass)
The iconic coastal mountain ride! Easy-rider tour (you ride pillion with a local driver — safest way). Stop at Elephant Springs for a swim in natural pools. Photo stops at the summit overlooking the coast. Arrive Hoi An late afternoon, wander lantern-lit Old Town at night ✨
Easy-rider: R700pp · Accom: R350 · Food: R200
Day 8
🏮 Hoi An — Old Town & Tailoring
Morning: Old Town walking tour — Japanese Covered Bridge, Assembly Halls, old merchant houses (Old Town ticket ~R75pp, valid 5 days). Get measured for tailored clothes first thing (suit ~R1,500-3,000, dress ~R800-2,000 — ready in 24-48h!). Afternoon: cooking class (market tour + cook 4 dishes). Evening: lantern boat ride on the river (~R30)
Accom: R350 · Old Town ticket: R75pp · Cooking class: R400pp · Tailoring: R1,500-3,000 · Food: R200
Day 9
🏮 Hoi An — Beach Day
Morning: An Bang Beach (~4km from town, free, rent lounger ~R30). Swim, read, chill. Afternoon: fitting for tailored clothes, final Hoi An wandering. Don't leave without trying banh mi (best in Vietnam!) and cao lau noodles — only made in Hoi An.
Accom: R350 · Lounger: R30 · Food: R200
Day 10
🏮 Hoi An → Pick Up Clothes + Sunrise Rice Paddies
Early morning: bike ride through rice paddies at sunrise (bike rental ~R30/d, magical light). Pick up tailored clothes — final fitting. Late morning: Grab to Danang airport. Fly Danang → Nha Trang (or Cam Ranh, ~1h flight).
Accom: R0 (fly out) · Bike: R30 · Flight: R900pp · Food: R200
Day 11
🏖️ Nha Trang — Beach & Mud Baths
Best July weather in Vietnam! ⭐ Morning: beach chill, swim, soak up the sun. Afternoon: Thap Ba mud baths (~R150pp) — uniquely Vietnamese, weird and wonderful. Evening: fresh seafood dinner on the beach (~R150-250pp).
Accom: R400 · Mud baths: R150pp · Food: R250
Day 12
🏖️ Nha Trang → Vinpearl or Chill Day
Option A: Vinpearl Land — amusement park on an island, cable car over the ocean is the highlight (~R600pp). Option B: Doc Let Beach day trip (40min north, pristine, quiet). Or just another beach day — no judgement! Evening: pack for HCMC.
Accom: R400 · Vinpearl: R600pp (optional) · Food: R200
Day 13
✈️ Nha Trang → Ho Chi Minh City
Fly Cam Ranh → HCMC (~1.5h, ~R900pp). Check into District 1 (backpacker area). Afternoon: War Remnants Museum (~R45pp) — powerful, essential visit. Evening: Bui Vien Street — backpacker bars, live music, cheap Bia Saigon. The energy is unreal!
Flight: R900pp · Accom: R400 · Museum: R45pp · Food: R200
Day 14
🌆 HCMC — Cu Chi Tunnels + City
Morning: Cu Chi Tunnels half-day tour (~R300pp incl. transport) — crawl through Viet Cong tunnels, try the dark tunnel experience. Afternoon: Ben Thanh Market (bargain hard! — start at 50%), Independence Palace, coffee culture crawl (ca phe sua da — Vietnamese iced coffee is life). Street food marathon for dinner.
Accom: R400 · Cu Chi: R300pp · Food: R200
Day 15
🛶 Mekong Delta → ✈️ Fly Home
Early morning: Mekong Delta day tour (~R350-500pp) — floating markets at dawn, boat through coconut groves, fruit orchards, local lunch. Back to HCMC by ~15:00. Evening flight HCMC → Doha → JHB. Last bowl of pho at the airport — see you next time Vietnam! 🇻🇳
Mekong tour: R400pp · Flight home: incl. in return ticket

Every Vietnam must-do packed into 10 days — beach day in Da Nang, no fluff, all killer
Hanoi food Ninh Binh caves Hai Van Pass Hoi An + HCMC Da Nang beach Fast pace

⚡ 10 days, zero fluff — now with a beach day! This is B1 on fast-forward — every highlight, no lazy days. You'll skip Nha Trang but get a proper beach afternoon at My Khe (Da Nang) — and you're 10min from the airport the next morning for your HCMC flight. Best for annual leave crushers.
Day 1
✈️ Fly JHB → Hanoi
Arrive Hanoi, get Viettel SIM (~R150) at airport, Grab to Old Quarter (~R80). Evening: Bun Cha Huong Lien (Obama's spot, ~R60 for 2) + Bia Hoi corner (draft beer ~R8/glass!). Get an early night — tomorrow's big.
SIM: R150 · Grab: R80 · Accom: R300 · Food: R200
Day 2
🏙️ Hanoi → Ninh Binh
Morning: quick Hanoi hits — egg coffee at Café Giang, Hoa Lo Prison (~1.5h). Midday: bus to Ninh Binh (~2h, ~R80pp). Afternoon: Trang An boat caves ⭐ (2-3h rowboat through limestone caves, UNESCO!). Rent scooter (~R100/d). Stay in Tam Coc.
Accom: R300 · Bus: R80pp · Scooter: R100 · Hoa Lo: R50pp · Trang An: R150pp · Food: R200
Day 3
🛖 Ninh Binh → 🚂 Sleeper Train to Hue
Early morning: Mua Caves sunrise (500 steps, stunning views, ~R75pp). Mid-morning: Bai Dinh Pagoda (largest in SE Asia, free). Afternoon: relax by the river, pack. Evening: sleeper train to Hue on Laman Express (~11h, ~R1,250pp). Pack snacks!
Train: R1,250pp · Mua Caves: R75pp · Scooter: R100 · Food: R200
Day 4
🏯 Hue → 🏍️ Hoi An (via Hai Van Pass)
Arrive Hue ~08:00. Quick Imperial City visit (2h, ~R100pp) — see the citadel and palace ruins. Then: easy-rider to Hoi An (~R700pp) via the Hai Van Pass ⭐ — one of the world's best coastal roads. Swim at Elephant Springs. Arrive Hoi An late afternoon. Evening: lantern-lit Old Town wander ✨
Imperial City: R100pp · Easy-rider: R700pp · Accom: R350 · Food: R200
Day 5
🏮 Hoi An — Old Town & Tailoring
Morning: Old Town walking tour — Japanese Covered Bridge, Assembly Halls (Old Town ticket ~R75pp). Get measured for tailored clothes ASAP (suit ~R1,500-3,000, dress ~R800-2,000, ready in 24-48h). Afternoon: cooking class (~R400pp) — market tour + cook 4 dishes. Evening: lantern boat on the river (~R30), banh mi & cao lau feast.
Accom: R350 · Old Town: R75pp · Cooking: R400pp · Tailoring: R1,500-3,000 · Food: R200
Day 6
🏖️ Hoi An → Da Nang (My Khe Beach)
Morning: pick up tailored clothes — final fitting. Midday: Grab to Da Nang (~45min). Check into a beachfront hotel on My Khe Beach — one of Vietnam's best city beaches, white sand, calm water ⭐. Afternoon: full beach chill, swim, seafood dinner on the sand. You're now 10min from Da Nang airport — no rush tomorrow!
Accom: R400 (beachfront) · Grab to Da Nang: R200 · Food: R200
Day 7
✈️ Da Nang → HCMC
Morning: last swim at My Khe if you're keen. Quick 10min Grab to Da Nang airport. Fly Da Nang → HCMC (~1.5h, ~R900pp). Check into District 1. Afternoon: War Remnants Museum (~R45pp) — powerful, essential. Evening: Bui Vien Street — bars, live music, Bia Saigon. The energy! 🔥
Flight: R900pp · Accom: R400 · Museum: R45pp · Food: R200
Day 8
🌆 HCMC — Cu Chi + City
Morning: Cu Chi Tunnels half-day tour (~R300pp) — crawl through Viet Cong tunnels, try the pitch-black section. Afternoon: Ben Thanh Market (bargain hard — start at 50%), Independence Palace, coffee culture crawl (ca phe sua da is life). Street food marathon for dinner — pho, banh mi, fresh spring rolls.
Accom: R400 · Cu Chi: R300pp · Food: R200
Day 9
🛶 Mekong Delta Day Trip
Early morning: Mekong Delta day tour (~R350-500pp) — floating markets at dawn (get there early!), boat through coconut groves, fruit orchards, local lunch. Back to HCMC by ~15:00. Evening: last Vietnamese dinner, pack, one more ca phe sua da.
Accom: R400 · Mekong tour: R400pp · Food: R200
Day 10
✈️ Fly Home
Morning: last bowl of pho, last Vietnamese coffee. HCMC → Doha → JHB. 10 days, 5 cities, zero regrets. See you next time Vietnam! 🇻🇳

✈️ Open-Jaw Strategy (recommended!)

All 3 itineraries go north → south. Fly into Hanoi and out of HCMC — no backtracking! Open-jaw tickets are often similar price to round-trip.

  • Option A & B: Fly JNB → HAN, fly HCMC → JNB
  • Option C: Fly JNB → DAD (Danang, via HCMC), fly HCMC → JNB
  • Internal flights are cheap — Vietjet/Bamboo ~R400-900 one-way
  • Sleeper trains save a hotel night + give you the scenic Reunification Express experience

⚠️ Weather Warnings

  • Trekking in Sapa/Ha Giang NOT advisable — very wet, muddy, poor visibility
  • Halong Bay cruises can be cancelled due to storms — have a backup plan
  • Central Vietnam (Hoi An, Nha Trang) is your best bet for great weather
  • South Vietnam is cheaper in low season — better accommodation deals
  • Pack rain gear regardless — even "dry" Central can get afternoon showers

✅ What's Great in July

  • Central Vietnam beaches are at their peak — Nha Trang, Hoi An, Danang
  • Fewer tourists in north & south = cheaper accommodation and no crowds
  • Rain in the south is usually short, dramatic afternoon bursts — rest of the day is fine
  • Lush green landscapes everywhere — best time for photography
  • Con Dao diving is still good (last month of dive season)

💸 Money-Saving Tips

  • Vietnam is very affordable — budget accommodation is clean and good value
  • Street food is incredible and costs R15-60 per meal
  • ATMs give better exchange rates than currency exchange counters
  • Internal flights (Vietjet, Bamboo) are cheap — ~R400-900 one-way
  • Grab (SE Asian Uber) is cheap and reliable — always use over flagged taxis
  • Haggling is expected at markets — start at 50% and negotiate up
  • Sleeper trains save a hotel night — book soft sleeper 4-berth minimum
  • 1 ZAR ≈ 1,400 VND (check current rate before you go)

🛂 Visa Requirements — South African Citizens

  • South Africans need a visa for Vietnam
  • E-visa available online: ~$25 USD (~R470) for 90 days single entry
  • E-visa 90 days multiple entry: ~$50 USD (~R940) — recommended if doing open-jaw flights
  • Apply at: evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn
  • Processing: ~3 working days
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months
  • No transit visa needed for Doha or Dubai layovers under 24h
📝 Apply for your e-visa at least 2-3 weeks before departure. Quick and straightforward — just need a passport photo and itinerary details.
