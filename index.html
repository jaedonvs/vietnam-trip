😴 Jet Lag Plan (Vietnam = +5h from JNB)

🌅 Day 1 strategy: Land HAN 13:55 local, stay up until at least 22:00 — the Old Quarter Night Market runs Sat nights and is one of Hanoi's best experiences. Perfect reason to fight the fatigue.
  • Outbound — sleep on JNB → DOH (8h): Take a melatonin 0.5–1mg ~30 min before boarding. Aim to sleep through as much of this leg as possible.
  • Outbound — stay awake DOH → HAN (7h): Watch films, walk the cabin, drink water, skip alcohol. Force yourself into Hanoi daytime so you arrive aligned.
  • Return — sleep SGN → DOH (7h): Aim to be up until 21:00 in SGN to make the evening flight feel natural. Sleep on the plane.
  • Return — sleep DOH → JNB (8h): Sleep again if you can. Arriving JNB 09:00 is brutal — straight home, bed, wake at sunset, then normal sleep that night.
  • Melatonin 0.5–1mg 30 min before target bedtime for the first 2–3 nights on the ground.
  • Hydration is the single biggest factor — drink water constantly on all 4 legs.

⚠️ Weather Warnings

  • Hanoi afternoon thunderstorms are loud but short — plan temple & mausoleum visits in the morning
  • Ha Long Bay cruises can be cancelled due to storms — have Ninh Binh (Trang An, Hoa Lu, Mua Cave) as backup
  • Hoi An & Da Nang are the best weather windows — protect against midday sun
  • HCMC rain pattern is reliable: clear mornings, downpours 14:00–17:00, clear again 19:00 — schedule Cu Chi + War Remnants Museum for AM
  • Pack rain gear regardless — even "dry" Central can get a passing shower

✅ What's Great in Late June

  • Hoi An & Da Nang beaches at their peak — My Khe in Da Nang, An Bang in Hoi An
  • Fewer tourists in Hanoi & HCMC = cheaper accommodation and no crowds
  • HCMC rain is usually short, dramatic afternoon bursts — evenings and mornings are perfect for walking streets
  • Lush green landscapes — best time for photography
  • Dragon Bridge fire show only happens Fri/Sat nights — you're there for both 🔥

💸 Money-Saving Tips

  • Vietnam is very affordable even at the "comfortable" tier — boutique hotels are still cheap by global standards
  • Street food is incredible and costs R15-60 per meal — mix with mid-range restaurants for variety
  • ATMs give better exchange rates than currency exchange counters
  • Internal flights (Vietjet, Bamboo) are cheap — ~R600-900 per sector (HAN→DAD, DAD→SGN)
  • Grab (SE Asian Uber) is cheap and reliable — always use over flagged taxis
  • Haggling is expected at markets — start at 50% and negotiate up
  • 1 ZAR ≈ 1,200 VND (check current rate before you go)
  • Boutique hotels in Old Quarter Hanoi & District 1 HCMC are a great value zone

🛂 Visa Requirements — South African Citizens

  • South Africans need a visa for Vietnam
  • E-visa available online: ~$25 USD (~R470) for 90 days single entry
  • E-visa 90 days multiple entry: ~$50 USD (~R940) — recommended if doing open-jaw flights
  • Apply at: evisa.gov.vn
  • Processing: ~3 working days
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months
  • No transit visa needed for Doha or Dubai layovers under 24h
📝 Apply for your e-visa at least 2-3 weeks before departure. Quick and straightforward — just need a passport photo and itinerary details.

💉 Health & Vaccinations

  • Hepatitis A & B recommended — standard for most of SE Asia
  • Typhoid recommended — especially if you'll eat street food (you will)
  • Consult a travel doctor 4-6 weeks before departure for boosters
  • Travel insurance must cover adventure activities — kayaking (Ha Long Bay), Cu Chi Tunnels crawling, motorbike trips
  • Pharmacies in Vietnam are well-stocked; bring basics (rehydration, painkillers, plasters)
  • Tap water: don't drink it. Use bottled or filtered.

Bun Cha Huong Lien
🍽️ Food
Obama & Anthony Bourdain ate here in 2016 — the bun cha spot that put Hanoi on the global food map. Grilled pork patties in sweet-sour broth with rice noodles and herbs. Plastic-stool local spot, no airs. Arrive at 12pm or join the queue.
~R80–140 per person
💡 Bourdain's table is preserved upstairs. Order the combo + a Hanoi beer.
Cafe Giang
☕ Cafe
The original Hanoi egg coffee since 1946. Whisky-yellow coffee made with whipped egg yolk and condensed milk, served hot or over ice. The recipe hasn't changed in 80 years.
~R25–45 per cup
💡 Order cà phê trứng nóng (hot) on a cool day, đá (iced) when it's hot. Both are perfect.
Hoan Kiem Lake + Ngoc Son Temple
🌅 View 🛕 Temple
The heart of Hanoi. Small lake in the old town, walking path around it, red bridge to Ngoc Son Temple on the island. Go on Day 1 afternoon while it's dry — beautiful in afternoon light. Locals doing tai chi at sunrise.
~R10 lake area · Temple free
💡 Sunrise tai chi scene is a different vibe from afternoon stroll — worth setting an alarm once.
Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum + One Pillar Pagoda
🏛️ History 🛕 Temple
HCM's preserved body in a Soviet-style mausoleum (closes at 11am, closed Mon/Fri). One Pillar Pagoda next door — Vietnam's most iconic Buddhist temple, built 1049, on a single stone pillar rising from a square pond.
Free
💡 Get there at 8am sharp. Modest dress required (shoulders + knees covered). Closed Mondays and Fridays.
Bia Hoi Corner (Ta Hien & Luong Ngoc Quyen)
🍽️ Food 🌃 Nightlife
Hanoi's most famous street corner. 25c draught beer (beverages) on plastic stools with locals. Open from mid-morning to late. The cheapest legal beer in Vietnam and a genuine cross-section of Hanoians.
~R6 per beer (yes, really)
💡 Go in the late afternoon — locals pile in from 5pm. A few of the spots have English menus, most don't. Point and enjoy.
Train Street
🌅 View 🥾 Adventure
The famous narrow street where trains pass through café tables. Access has been restricted on and off since 2019 — go and try. Worst case: you're on a great street with a delicious coffee. Best case: you watch a train roll past your cup.
Free · coffee ~R30–60
💡 Go during the day, ask cafés about train times. The experience is the attempt, not the certainty.

Cocobox
👗 Fashion ☕ Cafe 🛍️ Market
Concept store blending artisan fashion/boutique with a laid-back cafe. Locally-made clothing, textiles, jewellery, and homeware. Serves great Vietnamese coffee and light bites. Sustainable, traditional Vietnamese craftsmanship vibes.
Drinks ~R20–55 · Clothing ~R140–700
💡 Perfect for souvenirs that aren't mass-produced — grab unique pieces here
Banh Mi Phuong
🍽️ Food
Bold pick — arguably Vietnam's best banh mi. Anthony Bourdain named it in 2009. The queue out the door is the point. Pork pâté, cold cuts, fresh herbs, baguette shatter on a grill press. 3-minute wait for a lifetime of banh mi knowledge.
~R25–40 per sandwich
💡 Go at breakfast (before 9am) to skip the queue. Don't over-order — one is filling.
Quan Cao Iau Ba Le
🍽️ Food
The only place to eat authentic white rose dumplings (bánh bao vacuống) — a Hoi An specialty. Translucent shrimp dumplings shaped like roses, lightly steamed, dipped in a sweet-savoury fish sauce. Can ONLY be made properly in Hoi An because the well water gives the dough its unique texture.
~R80–140 for a plate of 8–10
💡 Order early in your Hoi An stay — they often sell out by 2pm.
Morning Glory Restaurant
🍽️ Food
The place to try Cao Lau — the noodle dish that can only properly be made in Hoi An (the water is the secret). Thick rice noodles, char siu pork, greens, crispy rice crackers, in a small amount of rich broth. Set in a colonial building, cooking-school pedigree.
~R120–200 per dish
💡 Book a cooking class here if interested (Red Bridge is the other top pick). The menu covers all central Vietnamese specialties.
Phuc Kien Assembly Hall (Fujian)
🛕 Temple 🌅 View
Bold pick — THE most beautiful temple in Hoi An. Chinese-Vietnamese assembly hall dedicated to the Goddess of the Sea. Enormous incense spirals hanging from the ceiling, ornate red altars, thick incense smoke, dragon carvings, lantern light. Go early when light comes through the entrance gate. Unmissable.
Entry included in Old Town ticket (~R120)
💡 Don't skip the inner courtyard — the real beauty is past the entrance. Light is best 8–10am.
Japanese Covered Bridge
🛕 Temple 🏛️ History
Hoi An's most iconic landmark — a 16th-century wooden bridge with a tiny temple inside, connecting the Japanese and Chinese quarters. Covered roof, weathered wood, two dog and two monkey statues guarding each end (the story behind them is dark and lovely).
Entry included in Old Town ticket
💡 Photographed best from the riverside at sunrise. Cross it both ways — the angles are very different.
Cam Thanh Coconut Village
🥾 Adventure 🌅 View
Mangrove palm forest a few km from Hoi An. The activity: bamboo basket boats (thúng chai), round woven boats, paddled by locals. Spinning tricks, fishing nets, a slow paddle through the mangrove tunnels. Genuinely fun, very photogenic.
~R200–350 pp for the boat experience
💡 Book a half-morning slot (8–11am or 2–5pm). Avoid the worst midday sun. Wear something you don't mind getting splashed.
An Bang Beach
🏖️ Beach 🍽️ Food
Hoi An's beach — 4km of clean sand, 4km from the Old Town, reached by bicycle in 15 min. Soul Kitchen is the famous beach bar: sun loungers, cold beer, wood-fired pizzas, sunset sessions. Quieter than My Khe in Da Nang.
Free · Sun lounger ~R50 · Beer ~R30
💡 Bicycle from Old Town (R50k VND/day rental). Go for sunset, not during peak heat.
Hoi An Night Market
🛍️ Market 🍽️ Food
Runs every night along Nguyen Hoang Street by the river. Lanterns lit everywhere, street food, souvenirs, tailoring tents, lanterns, ceramics, silk. The whole Old Town becomes this on summer evenings — atmospheric, busy, family-friendly.
Free · food ~R30–80 per item
💡 Combine with a lantern boat ride on the Thu Bon River (release a flower lantern together).

🌉 Da Nang12 places
Linh Ung Pagoda (Son Tra Peninsula)
🛕 Temple 🌅 View
Bold pick — giant Lady Buddha statue (67m tall) overlooking Da Nang Bay. Drive the full Son Tra Peninsula for panoramic views of the coast, jungle, and city. One of central Vietnam's most spectacular temple sites.
Free · ~R200 Grab from My Khe
💡 Combine with Marble Mountains in one morning — both are on the Da Nang side and ~30 mins apart
Marble Mountains
🛕 Temple 🥾 Adventure
Bold pick — Buddhist cave temples inside five limestone hills just south of Da Nang. Climb the steps, explore the caves, see pagodas tucked into the rock. Go before 10am to beat the heat and the tour buses.
~R70 entry + lift
💡 The caves are sheltered — works in any weather. Wear shoes with grip, the steps are uneven.
Ba Na Hills + Golden Bridge + French Village
🌅 View 🎢 Activity
World's longest single-cable carway up into the cloud forest. The Golden Bridge is the surreal one: giant stone hands cradling a pedestrian bridge above the mist. French Village replica + fantasy park at the top.
~R500 pp (book online in advance)
💡 Go afternoon — fewer crowds, better light. Plan 4–5 hours including cable car each way.
Dragon Bridge Fire & Water Show
🐉 Landmark 🌃 Nightlife
FREE show — the bridge breathes fire and shoots water from its dragon head. Runs Fri/Sat/Sun at 21:00. You're in Da Nang Fri 27 Jun AND Sat 28 Jun — both nights worth it. Position yourself on the Han River bank by 20:45.
Free
💡 The riverside bars on Bach Dang fill up fast. Get there by 20:30 for a good view with a drink.
My Khe Beach
🏖️ Beach
One of Southeast Asia's great city beaches — 30km of clean white sand right in Da Nang. Sunrise swim at 06:30 is nearly empty and golden. Sunset session with cold beer at Soul Kitchen-style beachfront bars is the move.
Free · Sun lounger ~R50
💡 Late June is the most reliable weather — full beach day. Beachfront seafood grills are excellent for dinner.
Mango Archive
👗 Vintage
Bold pick — Da Nang's best vintage clothing store. Curated second-hand pieces, retro sportswear, and one-off finds. The kind of place you walk out with three things you didn't know you needed.
~R150–800 per piece
💡 Hit it on the An Thuong Street vintage strip — pair with Rust Rack, Hana 34, ORI jewellery in one walk
An Thuong Street Vintage Strip
👗 Vintage ☕ Cafes
The full vintage & thrift strip near My Khe Beach — Mango Archive, Rust Rack, Hana 34, ORI jewellery, plus dozens of independent shops. Cafes and bars mixed in. Plan a 2-3 hour browse.
Varies — R100–1,500
💡 Walk the whole strip once before buying — you'll find the best pieces on the second loop
Han River Cruise
🌅 View 🌃 Nightlife
Dinner cruise or simple boat ride on the Han River — see Da Nang lit up from the water, with the Dragon Bridge as the centrepiece. Runs nightly, several operators along Bach Dang waterfront.
~R350–700 pp with dinner
💡 Book the 19:00 slot to see the bridges lit up before the Dragon Bridge show at 21:00.
Son Tra Peninsula Drive
🌅 View 🥾 Adventure
Scenic 30km loop around the Son Tra Peninsula — jungle, ocean views, Linh Ung Pagoda at the peak, hidden beaches, the occasional wild monkey. Rent a scooter or hire a Grab for a half-day. The single best thing to do in Da Nang if you have a window.
Free · scooter ~R150k VND/day
💡 Drive the loop clockwise for the best views. Stop at the multiple viewpoints — there are at least 5 worth pulling over for.
Rust Rack
👗 Vintage
Another An Thuong Street vintage pick — curated second-hand with a more streetwear / workwear lean than Mango Archive. Good for denim and overshirts.
~R150–700 per piece
Hana 34
💍 Vintage
Handmade jewellery on the An Thuong strip — delicate pieces, mostly gold and silver, with a focus on minimalist Vietnamese design. Good for gifts.
~R100–800 per piece
Mi Quang (Da Nang's signature)
🍽️ Food
The dish Da Nang is most proud of — turmeric-coloured broth, rice noodles, pork and prawn, peanuts, sesame rice crackers, fresh herbs. Served in small portions as a snack or main. Many local spots, look for the steam coming off the pots.
~R50–100 per bowl
💡 Eat it at a local spot with plastic stools, not a tourist restaurant. The dish is best when it's been on the burner for hours.

Hardy Boys

Deeritage
💍 Vintage
Vintage jewelry shop with a curated collection of unique rings, necklaces, and accessories — thrifted gems and one-of-a-kind pieces. Like treasure hunting but make it fashion. 18/4 Trần Quý Khoách, District 1.
~R40–400 per piece
💡 Open 9:30am–10pm daily — rings start as low as R40!
Rue Miche
👗 Fashion
Multi-brand fashion boutique housed in a restored 1952 French colonial villa — 25+ curated local brands under one roof. Think concept-store energy with independent Vietnamese designers. Also has a healthy beverage bar. 9B Phùng Khắc Khoan, District 1.
~R300–1,500 per piece
💡 Also has Rue Miche L'Édition at Union Square (171 Đồng Khởi) — bigger flagship space
Cu Chi Tunnels
🏛️ History 🥾 Adventure
80km of Viet Cong tunnel network used during the Vietnam War. Half-day tour from HCMC — crawl through the tunnels (now widened for tourists), see the trapdoors, the kitchens, the field hospital. Fire a rifle range at the end (extra). Powerful, intense, sometimes heavy. Small group tour recommended.
~R400–600 pp (~$20–30)
💡 Depart 07:30, back by 13:00. Morning-only is non-negotiable for the HCMC weather — afternoons are heavy rain.
War Remnants Museum
🏛️ History
The most visceral museum in Vietnam. US military equipment on the lawn, photo exhibitions inside that are unflinching about the war's human cost. Powerful, often upsetting, essential. Closes 17:00 — go straight from the airport on Day 9.
~R40 entry
💡 Allow 2–3 hours. The Agent Orange exhibition on the upper floor is the hardest part. Bring water, sit in the garden if it gets too much.
Reunification Palace
🏛️ History
The presidential palace of South Vietnam, taken over by a North Vietnamese army tank on 30 April 1975. The tank is still on the front lawn. Inside: 1960s furnishings, the war command room, the presidential dining room, the rooftop helipad. Time capsule.
~R40 entry
💡 Combine with the War Remnants Museum in one afternoon. The two together are 4–5 hours and you understand the war from both sides.
Bo Ne Ba Nui
🍽️ Food
Bold pick — the $4 Vietnamese 'steak and eggs' breakfast. Beef, eggs, onions, pate, tomatoes sizzling on a cast-iron skillet. Add a fried egg on top. Local institution, simple plastic-stool setting. Goes back twice on Day 11 — once is not enough.
~R70–130 per person
💡 Go first thing in the morning — that's when the beef is freshest. Order one per person + a side of banh mi.
Pho Viet Nam
🍽️ Food
Bold pick — your HCMC pho spot for Day 10 dinner. Southern pho is sweeter and more herb-forward than Hanoi's, with bean sprouts, basil, lime, hoisin & sriracha on the side. The original is the one Bourdain visited in 2002.
~R80–140 per bowl
💡 Add the rare beef (tái) — it's the move.
Quan Bui Garden Restaurant
🍽️ Food
Traditional Vietnamese in a gorgeous colonial courtyard — wooden shutters, hanging lanterns, tropical greenery. One of the best meals of the trip. The menu covers the full Vietnamese canon: pho, bun, com, goi cuon, all done well.
~R300–600 pp with drinks
💡 Book ahead for the courtyard tables at dinner. The upstairs section is air-conditioned.
Pasteur Street Brewing Company
🍽️ Food 🌃 Nightlife
Bold pick — HCMC's best craft brewery. The perfect FINAL BEER of the trip. Vietnamese-inspired beers brewed with local ingredients: jasmine IPA, dragon fruit saison, lemongrass wit, passionfruit pale ale. Order the tasting paddle (4–6 beers), raise a glass, and reflect on 12 days done.
~R150–250 for a tasting paddle
💡 District 1, easy Grab from dinner. Don't drink and fly — book the paddle for the night before departure.
The Refinery
🍽️ Food
French-Vietnamese fusion in a former opium refinery — exposed brick, wooden beams, the building alone is worth the booking. Bistro classics done well: steak frites, duck confit, creme brulee. The farewell-dinner move for a couple.
~R600–1,000 pp with wine
💡 Book ahead for the garden tables. Pair the tasting menu with a French wine.
EON51 at Bitexco Tower
🍽️ Food 🌅 View
Floor 51 of the Bitexco Financial Tower — floor-to-ceiling windows, 360° city views, Vietnamese-European menu. Both a farewell lunch option (Day 12) and a special-occasion dinner. The lunch is the value move — a long meal, a view, and you walk out under budget for dinner.
~R600–1,200 pp for lunch
💡 Book the 13:00 lunch slot on Day 12. Dress smart — they hold the dress code for the dining room.
