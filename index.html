💡 Flight Tips

  • Hanoi is ~R800-1,500 cheaper than HCMC — consider flying into HAN and out of SGN
  • Saturday departures tend to be the cheapest day to fly
  • Hacker Fares on KAYAK (mix airlines) can save money
  • Qatar Airways economy seats are tight — pack light, consider seat selection fee
  • No transit visa needed for Doha or Dubai layovers under 24h
  • Book 6 weeks ahead (mid-May) to save ~12% vs last-minute

⚠️ Weather Warnings

  • Trekking in Sapa/Ha Giang NOT advisable — very wet, muddy, poor visibility
  • Halong Bay cruises can be cancelled due to storms
  • Central Vietnam (Hoi An, Nha Trang) is your best bet for great weather
  • South Vietnam is cheaper in low season — better accommodation deals
  • Pack rain gear regardless — even "dry" Central can get afternoon showers

💸 Money-Saving Tips

  • Vietnam is very affordable — budget accommodation is clean and good value
  • Street food is incredible and costs R30-60 per meal
  • ATMs give better exchange rates than currency exchange counters
  • Internal flights (Vietjet, Bamboo) are cheap — ~R400-800 one-way
  • Grab (Southeast Asian Uber) is cheap and reliable
  • Haggling is expected at markets — start at 50% and negotiate up

🛂 Visa Requirements — South African Citizens

  • South Africans need a visa for Vietnam
  • E-visa available online: ~$25 USD (~R470) for 90 days, single entry
  • Apply at: evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn
  • Processing: ~3 working days
  • Passport must be valid for at least 6 months
  • No transit visa needed for Doha or Dubai layovers under 24h
📝 Apply for your e-visa at least 2-3 weeks before departure. It's quick and straightforward — just need a passport photo and itinerary details.
